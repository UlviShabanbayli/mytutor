# Motion — approved animations

The user reviewed 65 animation samples in the motion lab (`motion-lab.html`, published as the
Artifact "arı Hərəkət Laboratoriyası") and approved one variant of each on 2026-10-20; four were
reworked after notes (hive.crystallize, hive.seal, micro.timer, transitions.tabs) and then approved.

- `motion-decisions.json`: every sample with all variants, the chosen one and the user's note.
- `motion-lab.html`: the lab itself (open it in a browser to replay any variant).
- Shared values live in `apps/mobile/src/motion` (durations, easings, springs, reduced motion);
  haptics go through `playCue` in `apps/mobile/src/lib/cues.ts`.

Rules that hold for every animation:

- Reduced motion (the phone's setting or Mən → Ayarlar → "Hərəkəti azalt") shows the end state or a
  150 ms fade; nothing travels, bounces or loops.
- A wrong answer never shakes, flashes red or plays a loud cue.
- Vızı appears at most once per screen and never inside a test question.
- No confetti; celebrations use honey, cells and Vızı.
